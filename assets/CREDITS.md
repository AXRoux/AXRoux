# Credits

Every card on this profile is drawn by `scripts/build.mjs` from the GitHub API, with no third-party card service, and
refreshed daily by `.github/workflows/refresh.yml`.

- **Fonts:** [Geist and Geist Mono](https://github.com/vercel/geist-font) by Vercel, SIL Open Font License 1.1
  (`fonts/LICENSE.txt`), subset to Latin and embedded in each image.
- **Company logos:** [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons) (MIT) for the AI companies, in their brand
  colours; [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) (CC0 1.0) for SpaceX, Stripe, Visa, Telegram,
  TypeScript, Python, X and GitHub; the Airwallex mark as used in [Tessera](https://github.com/AXRoux/tessera).
- **Card art:** holographic plates generated with [fal.ai](https://fal.ai) (Flux Pro 1.1 Ultra), in `art/`. The profile
  card is my own photo, upscaled with Real-ESRGAN and cut out with BiRefNet onto a generated foil plate.
